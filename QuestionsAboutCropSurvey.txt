2. Mismatch of crop type between OSE table and xirrigcu crop type:
	1. GB - Garden Beans. The OSE table lists this as ID 703. Our xirrigcu JSON library has "12: Dry Beans" and "3: Misc. Vegetable". I assume it should relate to BC_ID 3 (Misc. Vegetable). The 700-series in the OSE table typically represents fresh vegetables, making BC_ID 3 a more scientifically accurate fit than Dry Beans.

	2. PI - Pistachios. The OSE table lists this as 610, adjacent to Pecans (609). Our JSON library has "4: Orchard" and "27: Pecan Orchards". I assume it goes to BC_ID 4 (Orchard). Pecans (27) have highly unique water-use curves in NM. Pistachios likely align closer to standard orchard parameters rather than pecan-specific curves.

	3. PEW - Pecans WUCB. The OSE table lists this as 609.1, a subset of Pecans. I guess it goes to BC_ID 27 (Pecan Orchards). Since it is a pecan variety, it should share the same parameters as standard Pecans.

	4. WSG - Winter Small Grains. The OSE table specifies "Winter Small Grains (Spring) - 140". Our JSON library has two options: "8: Winter Small Grain (winter-spring)" and "9: Winter Small Grain (fall)". I think it goes to BC_ID 8 (Winter Small Grain, winter-spring). This directly aligns with the "(Spring)" designation explicitly mentioned in the OSE table.

	5. SSL - Swamped/Seeped Land. Present in both GIS and OSE tables. I think it should be BC_ID 0 (Excluded from standard CIR calculations). While seeped lands consume water via evaporation, for the purpose of the Crop Survey and Decree accounting, I assume they are treated similarly to Fallow (FA) or Out Areas (OUT) and are not calculated as artificially irrigated acreage requiring a BC_ID.
